NIVAROA Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

NIVAROA is designed for aesthetic clarity, live weather skies, and serene home screen organization. We respect your privacy and prioritize local-first data processing. This Privacy Policy explains how NIVAROA handles information when you use our Android launcher.

1. Information Collection & Permissions

To provide immersive weather canvases, interactive widgets, and launcher functionality, NIVAROA requests specific device permissions:

  • • Location Data (Coarse/Fine): Used exclusively to fetch local weather forecasts from MET Norway to render live weather particle skies and celestial solar phases. Coordinates are transmitted securely and never stored or shared for advertising.
  • • Installed Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Queried locally on your device to display your installed applications in the categorized app drawer and dock.
  • • Calendar Events (READ_CALENDAR): Used locally to display upcoming schedule events in the interactive Month Calendar and Hourly Weather Timeline widgets. Calendar data is never uploaded or transmitted.
  • • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used locally for the Biometric Hidden App Vault to secure sensitive apps behind your device's fingerprint or face unlock.

2. Local-First Data Storage

Your sticky notes, tasks, calendar highlights, and launcher preferences are stored locally on your hardware in a secure local database. We do not maintain external cloud servers for your personal notes or app usage data.

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

NIVAROA integrates standard third-party services to support core features and optional monetization:

  • • Google AdMob: Displays banner ads and rewarded ads (used for temporary 15-minute feature passes). AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage metrics in accordance with Google's privacy policies.
  • • Google Play Billing: Securely processes in-app purchases and subscriptions for NIVAROA Pro. Financial transactions are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • • MET Norway Weather API: Processes anonymous location queries to return live meteorological conditions.

4. Your Control & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data:

  • • In-App Management: You can clear sticky notes, reset preferences, or modify vault settings directly within NIVAROA Settings.
  • • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application permanently removes the local database and all associated device records.
  • • Privacy Inquiries: You may contact us regarding data privacy at contact@dattara.app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or NIVAROA's data practices, please reach out to: contact@dattara.app
