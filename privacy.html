General Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Welcome to DATTARA APPS. We engineer tools for digital intentionality and focus. Our core philosophy is simple: your data belongs to you. We do not actively collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information from your device across any of our applications.

1. Company-Wide Commitment

This General Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications and associated services operated by DATTARA APPS. While specific applications may have additional requirements to function as intended, the fundamental principle remains: we do not profit from your personal data.

2. Local-First Processing

Whenever possible, our applications process information locally on your hardware. We believe that privacy is not just a feature, but a structural requirement for focused engineering.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications may use essential third-party services (such as Google Play Services) to ensure stability, facilitate licensing, and provide crash reporting. These services may collect anonymous technical data as part of their standard operations.

4. Future Applications

As we expand the Dattara ecosystem, every new tool will be built with this same commitment to privacy and non-collection of personally identifiable information.

5. Contact Us

For general inquiries regarding our privacy standards, please contact us at: contact@dattara.app
