AirgapMail

🛡️ 100% On-Device Email Cleaner & Security Vault

Reclaim your inbox from promotional clutter, ghost subscriptions, and commercial tracking. AirgapMail processes every single email rule locally on your Android device with zero cloud logging or remote server dependencies.

🛡️ Built on "Air-Gapped" On-Device Security Principles

AirgapMail is engineered from the ground up for strict privacy, local performance, and zero data leakage.

🚫 Zero Remote Cloud Logging

Dattara Apps operates no intermediate cloud servers or tracking databases. Your emails, metadata, and credentials never touch our servers.

🔒 SQLCipher On-Device Vault

All local caches, rule indexes, and token references are stored exclusively on your Android device in an encrypted Room database using SQLCipher and Android Security Crypto.

⚡ Direct Provider OAuth 2.0

OAuth 2.0 PKCE authentication connects your device directly to official Google (gmail.googleapis.com) or Microsoft Graph endpoints over secure TLS.

🧹 Smart Newsletter Purge

Identify inactive promotional lists, bulk purge junk subscriptions, and execute trashing or archiving commands on your direct instruction.

Google API Services & Limited Use Policy Compliance

AirgapMail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The requested scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify is used solely on-device to execute email cleanup operations (trashing or archiving promotional mail) explicitly triggered by the user inside the application.
