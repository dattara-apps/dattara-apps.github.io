Dattara Apps built the Dina Panchang app as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided by Dattara Apps at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

  • Location Data (Coarse Location Only): Dina Panchang requests access to approximate device location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) solely to calculate regional astronomical positions, sunrise/sunset times, and local Panchang muhurtas. Location data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to external servers or shared with third parties.
  • Diagnostic & Crash Logs: Anonymous error logs and performance diagnostics may be collected via standard app store analytics to help us improve stability and fix bugs.

Permissions Used

  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required for calculating regional astronomical timings and Panchang.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to schedule timely notifications for festivals and daily Panchang alerts.
  • INTERNET: Required for fetching ephemeris calculation updates, timezone data, and city search queries.
